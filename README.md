# 📊 Customer Retention & Churn Analysis Dashboard

This project is a **self-driven data analytics and business intelligence project** created to strengthen my **Power BI**, **customer analytics**, and **data visualization** skills using a real-world public dataset.

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer retention and churn behavior using Power BI to identify the factors that influence customer loyalty and the reasons customers leave a subscription-based business.

The objective was to transform raw customer subscription data into an interactive dashboard that helps business stakeholders understand churn patterns, customer lifetime trends, and key retention drivers through actionable insights and visual storytelling.

---

## Project Objective

Analyze customer subscription data to identify:

- Customer churn patterns
- Retention trends
- High-risk customer segments
- Customer lifetime behavior
- Service and contract impact on churn
- Business strategies to improve customer retention

## Key Insights

### 1. Contract Analysis

- Customers with **Month-to-Month contracts** exhibit the highest churn rate.
- Longer contract durations significantly improve customer retention.
- Two-Year contract customers show the highest average tenure.

### 2. Internet Service Analysis

- **Fiber Optic customers** have the highest churn rate among all internet services.
- Customers without Device Protection or Tech Support are more likely to leave.
- Additional value-added services improve customer retention.

### 3. Payment Method Analysis

- **Electronic Check** users represent the highest-risk payment segment.
- Customers using automatic payment methods tend to stay longer.
- Paperless Billing customers exhibit higher churn behavior.

### 4. Demographics Analysis

- **Senior Citizens** have the highest demographic churn rate.
- Customers without partners or dependents are more likely to churn.
- Gender has relatively little impact on customer retention.

### 5. Retention Analysis

- Most customer churn occurs during the **first year** of service.
- Customer loyalty increases as tenure grows.
- Long-term customers (4+ years) demonstrate the strongest retention.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are suggested:

- **Encourage customers to switch from Month-to-Month contracts** by offering discounts for annual or long-term plans.
- **Improve Fiber Optic service quality** and provide bundled support packages.
- **Promote automatic payment methods** through cashback offers and loyalty incentives.
- **Develop targeted retention campaigns** for Senior Citizens and customers without family commitments.
- **Strengthen first-year onboarding programs** to reduce early customer churn.
- **Bundle value-added services** such as Device Protection and Tech Support to improve customer loyalty.
